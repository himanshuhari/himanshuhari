## About Me

Hi, I’m **Himanshu Hari**, pursuing **MS IN QUANTITATIVE ECONOMICS** from *Indian Statistical Institute(Delhi)* after completing my  **Bachelor of Science in Economic Sciences** at the *Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal*, India along a **minor in Data Science and Engineering**.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of **applied microeconomic theory**, **macroeconomics**, **public finance**, **economic policy**, and **development economics**. I'm deeply curious about the mechanisms and institutions that shape societies and drive national outcomes—what *Daron Acemoglu* explores in *Why Nations Fail*. I’m particularly interested in understanding the algorithmic and systemic structures that create long-term economic success or stagnation.

I also enjoy working with **time series**,**econometrics**, **machine learning**, and **causal inference**, especially in the context of public policy and economic analysis. What fascinates me is not just applying tools, but understanding *why and how* the math behind these tools works—and how rigorous models, when combined with high-quality data, can produce persuasive, impactful, and actionable insights.
